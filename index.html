About

Technical depth from academia, applied on site.

I am a civil engineer moving from structural academia and research into full-time industry practice. I hold a 2026 MSc in Civil Construction and Real Estate Management from Riga Technical University (Distinction) and a Master’s in Structural Engineering.

My grounding covers structural design software, concrete technology and construction quality assessment, backed by peer-reviewed research and patented innovation.

Teaching and research gave me depth in structural behaviour, concrete technology and quality assessment. My site work at Shereasava Enterprises and my MSc in Riga are where I started applying that depth to delivery: checking work against design intent, coordinating subcontractors and keeping programmes on track. I am now moving into industry to do this full time, in a junior- to mid-level civil or structural engineering role where technical depth and a fast learning curve add value.

Case Studies

Three projects, told as Situation, Task, Action and Result.

Site delivery · 2024–25

RCC works on an 8-storey apartment building

Situation
Shereasava Enterprises was third-party contractor for the RCC works of an 8-storey apartment building in Pune, one of two sites (with a G+1 bungalow) worth ₹5 Cr+ together.
Task
Manage the RCC work on three of the eight storeys, keeping it compliant with design, safety and programme requirements.
Action
Ran site inspections, coordinated subcontractors and resources, resolved technical issues, and used look-ahead planning and progress reporting.
Result
Contributed to a 20% reduction in project delays.
Structural analysis · 2023–24

45-storey RCC frame with outriggers

Situation
Tall reinforced concrete frames face large lateral deflection, and outrigger placement is a key design decision.
Task
Show how outrigger configurations reduce deflection compared with conventional RC systems.
Action
Compared several outrigger placements using concrete and steel-braced trusses, against frames with and without shear walls.
Result
A documented comparison of configurations, registered as copyright L-150695/2024.
Quality assessment · 2014–15

Combined non-destructive testing (SONREB)

Situation
Concrete strength in existing structures needs to be checked without damaging them. The project was sponsored by Constrologix Engineering & Research Services.
Task
Test whether combined non-destructive methods agree with cube compressive strength.
Action
Compared combined NDT readings on laboratory specimens and existing buildings against cube compression tests.
Result
2nd prize at the state-level INNOVATION–2015 competition, presented at ICBCCE-2015 and published as a 2015 journal paper.
